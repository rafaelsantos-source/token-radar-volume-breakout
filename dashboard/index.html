How a row is valued

implied = TVL × 0.30 × momentum × maturity × breadth × tier

momentum = 1 + 0.55 × clamp(7d change, −70%, +100%) / 100 · maturity grows with days listed (unknown: 365) · breadth rewards extra chains · tier is the cohort’s own 7-day growth distribution: BREAKOUT at or above the 90th percentile, HEATING at or above the 75th. A +4,448% print is clamped to +100%: a data spike is not a breakout. Nothing here names a protocol specially — the same function scores every row.
